# Support

## Where to get help

### Bug reports
If you found a bug, please open a [bug report](https://github.com/versus184-py/Mosaic/issues/new?labels=bug&template=bug.yml). Include your OS, Mosaic version, and steps to reproduce.

### Feature requests
Suggest ideas via a [feature request](https://github.com/versus184-py/Mosaic/issues/new?labels=enhancement&template=feature.yml).

### Questions & discussion
General Q&A, ideas, and conversation happen in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/versus184-py/Mosaic/discussions). This is the best place for "how do I..." questions or brainstorming.

### Documentation
Full documentation lives on the [wiki](https://github.com/versus184-py/Mosaic/wiki). Start with:
- [Installation Guide](https://github.com/versus184-py/Mosaic/wiki/Installation-Guide)
- [Troubleshooting & FAQ](https://github.com/versus184-py/Mosaic/wiki/Troubleshooting-and-FAQ)

### Security vulnerabilities
Please report security issues privately — see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) for the disclosure process.

### Community
Be kind. Follow our [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
